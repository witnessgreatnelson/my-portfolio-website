About Me

Hi, I am Nelson Elemi, an aspiring Software Developer based in Lagos, Nigeria.

I am currently studying Software Development through BYU-Pathway Worldwide (BYU-Idaho), building a strong foundation in web design, programming, and full-stack development. Alongside my studies, I am learning hands-on with freeCodeCamp and building real projects as I learn.

I help small businesses, churches, and community projects get online with simple, fast, mobile-friendly websites. My focus is on clean design, clear information, and websites that work well on phones - because that is how most people in Lagos browse.

My background in community and health outreach gives me a unique advantage: I understand how to communicate clearly, listen to people's needs, and build solutions that actually help them. I bring that same care into every website I build.

Right now, I am focused on mastering HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and growing into a full-stack developer who can build complete web applications from start to finish.

What I do:

  • Business Websites and Landing Pages
  • Portfolio and Personal Websites
  • Websites for Churches and Community Projects
  • Health Information Pages

My goal: To become a professional software developer and use technology to help businesses grow and communities thrive in Nigeria and beyond.
